Free field-day app — built by a former school administrator

Hey! It's Richard. I spent 26 years as a school administrator in Ontario and international schools before going back to the classroom — and I built Curriculate Field Day after running too many of my own schools' field days on spreadsheets. It's what I wished I'd had every June.

It's in active pilot phase. We're inviting a small number of schools to use it free during R&D in exchange for thoughtful feedback after their field day.

What it actually does (the stuff that matters on field-day morning):

  • Multi-runner stopwatch, hundredth-second precision, taps to start/stop heats
  • Placement scoring and standards-based scoring side-by-side
  • Records & PBs trigger an air-horn on the leader's phone — kids love it
  • Houses, divisions, heats, relays — out of the box
  • Import your Excel roster, print 1″×1″ Avery ribbon labels
