Hey! It's Richard. After more than 25 years in education across Ontario and international schools — including teaching and school leadership roles — I went back into the classroom. I built Curriculate Field Day after running too many of my own schools' field days on spreadsheets — it's what I wished I'd had every June.

Pilot Program: active R&D phase — Field Day is free of charge in exchange for thoughtful feedback after your event. Nothing to download, nothing to buy.

What it actually does (the stuff that matters on field-day morning):

  • Multi-runner stopwatch, hundredth-second precision, taps to start/stop heats
  • Placement scoring and standards-based scoring side-by-side
  • New records & PBs trigger an air-horn on the leader's phone — kids love it
  • Houses, divisions, heats, relays — out of the box
  • Import your Excel roster, print 1″×1″ Avery ribbon labels

Most schools can be fully set up in under 20 minutes from an Excel roster.
