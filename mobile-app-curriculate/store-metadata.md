## Full Description

**Turn any lesson into a live, interactive game your class will actually remember.**

Curriculate is the teacher's game master. Describe a lesson and Curriculate builds interactive task stations — quizzes, sorts, mind maps, role plays, hangman duels, and more — then runs the whole thing as a live session your students join from any device.

**What Curriculate does:**
• Describe a lesson → AI builds a ready-to-play scavenger hunt
• 20+ interactive task types: multiple choice, VennSort, mind mapper, script play, speech recognition, and more
• Run live sessions students join with a code
• Real-time leaderboard and progress as students play
• Diagnose & Fix keeps every generated task clean and playable
• Works on the teacher's phone as the game master console
• Instant camera + QR for joining and photo tasks
• Haptic celebration moments as students score

**Who it's for:**
• Teachers who want lessons that move
• Substitute teachers who need an instant, self-running activity
• Any classroom from elementary to high school

**Free to start.** Open the app, describe your lesson, and run your first session in minutes.
