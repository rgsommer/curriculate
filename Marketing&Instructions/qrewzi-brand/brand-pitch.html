Brand pitch · v1 · Aug 2026

The classroom becomes the game.

Qrewzi is the kid-facing rebrand of Curriculate's live-classroom product — a QR-driven team scavenger hunt engine that turns any lesson into a 30-minute room-wide game. This page lays out the mark options, wordmark treatments, palette, and typography.

The Mark

01 · three directions

Every direction anchors on the QR-code finder pattern — the black square with a smaller filled square inside that phone cameras look for. It's instantly recognized worldwide, and it happens to look like a Q. That's the thesis: the letter and the mechanic are the same shape.

A Recommended
Finder + Tail
Full QR finder pattern with a rounded diagonal kicker for the Q's tail. Reads as both symbol and letter — the strongest dual duty.
B
Pure Finder
The finder pattern alone. Feels more like a system icon than a letter. Excellent as a favicon; weaker as a standalone mark.
C
Pixel Q
A bold Q letterform with QR-pixel array inside the counter. Reads letter-first. Playful, but more work to render cleanly at small sizes.

The Wordmark

02 · three treatments

Mark A becomes the Q; Unbounded 800 carries "rewzi" with a tight −2 letter-spacing so the finder pattern locks visually into the word. Three treatments cover the daily use cases.

In Use

03 · applications

The mark shrinks cleanly. At favicon size the tail vanishes — the pure finder pattern takes over. The app icon inverts to cream-on-coral so it pops on any home-screen wallpaper.

Palette

04 · five roles

Coral leads because kid-facing edtech is crowded with purples, blues, and pastels — coral+navy stands out on a shelf. Butter yellow is the spark reserved for interactive moments (buttons pressed, correct answers, superpower reveals) so it never wears out.
