🎯 Pulse Grading

AI grading for teachers. Photograph, paste, or batch-upload a whole class PDF; pick a rubric; get scored feedback in seconds. Handles handwritten work, video and audio performance, and exports to Edsby.

🎮 Curriculate Live Sessions

AI-generated in-classroom scavenger hunts. Teachers describe a topic; Curriculate builds a live team game with 30+ task types. Kids play on any device while the teacher runs a GameMaster projector dashboard.

The Problem

Teachers grade nights and weekends. A single class set can eat 2–4 hours; the feedback loop for students stretches to a week.
Lecture-mode loses students fast. Traditional whole-class instruction disengages most kids within 15 minutes.
EdTech is fragmented. Teachers stitch together six tools that don't talk to each other — nothing bridges assessment and active learning.

The Solution — one platform, two workflows

Pulse Grading turns 3 hours of grading into 20 minutes with rubric-matched AI feedback teachers can trust.
Live Sessions turn any lesson into a 30-minute team game — 30+ interactive task types spanning all six Bloom's levels.
Shared class roster means grades from Live Sessions flow directly into per-student progress reports for parents.
