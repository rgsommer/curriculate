## Full Description (both stores, 4000 max)

Join the game. Play the lesson.

Qrewzi turns your classroom into a live team game. Your teacher shows a room code — enter it, name your team, and start playing interactive task stations right on your phone or tablet. No account, no setup, no waiting.

What you can do:
• Join a live game with a room code — no account needed
• Play 20+ task types: quizzes, sorts, matching, mind maps, sequences, role plays, and more
• Scan station QR codes to move around the room
• Record answers, take photos, and complete speaking and movement challenges
• Watch your team climb the live leaderboard
• Chase a secret team superpower — only some teams get one!

Made for the classroom:
• Works on iPhone, iPad, and Android phones and tablets
• Fast join-by-code — you're playing in seconds
• Built for class sets of shared devices

Just want to look around? Tap "Try a demo game" on the join screen — no code or account required.

Ask your teacher for a room code and jump in.
