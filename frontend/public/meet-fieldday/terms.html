What Field Day is

A free web app from Curriculate for running a school field day or track meet — timing events, scoring competitors, tracking records and personal bests, and printing ribbons. Hosted at curriculate.net/fieldday.

Who can use it

Field Day is intended for staff at schools (administrators, teachers, coaches, parent volunteers) acting in their official school role. The school agrees to use the app for the purpose of running its own events and not for any commercial purpose.

What you can do

  • Create a school in the app and run as many field days, track meets, or athletic events as you'd like.
  • Add as many admins, leaders, and competitors as you need.
  • Export your data at any time, in any quantity, in JSON or Excel format.
  • Recommend the app to other schools using the in-app referral feature.
  • Suggest features or report problems via the in-app Report button.
