Who We Are

A boutique developer with the capability of a much larger firm.

Street Properties is a boutique development group dedicated to enhancing communities through value-driven commercial projects.

Our full scope of in-house services includes unmatched expertise in the areas of site identification, land acquisition, land use planning and government approvals, development and construction, property management, and maintenance services. From creating new commercial developments to acquiring, improving and revitalizing existing shopping centres, Street Properties develops or repositions a range of asset classes throughout Ontario, with a focus on the GTA.

Patient capital. Hands-on stewardship. Enduring value.
