Our Story

A privately held developer with a builder's mindset.

Street Properties has spent more than three decades creating and caring for commercial and residential real estate across the Greater Toronto Area.

Since the early 1990s

Boutique by choice, comprehensive by design.

We are deliberately small — a focused group that takes on a select number of projects and gives each one the attention it deserves.

From our office on Avenue Road in Toronto, we identify opportunities, assemble land, secure approvals, and build. Then we stay. Owning and managing what we develop keeps us honest: every decision we make today is one we'll live with for years to come.

That long horizon shapes everything — the tenants we court, the materials we specify, the relationships we keep with municipalities and communities. We aren't here to flip; we're here to build value that lasts.

We build it, we keep it, we stand behind it.

In-House Capability

Everything we need, on our own team.

Most projects pass through a dozen hands. Ours stay within one team — and that changes the outcome.

Because site selection, acquisition, planning, approvals, construction, leasing, and management all live under one roof, information never gets lost in translation. The person who chose the site is still in the room when we set the tenant mix and when we hand a unit over to a retailer.

The result is a developer that thinks like an owner, builds like a craftsman, and manages like a neighbour.
