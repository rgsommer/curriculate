What We Do

The full development lifecycle, handled in-house.

Seven disciplines, one accountable team — from finding the right parcel of land to keeping a thriving centre at its best for decades.

Capabilities

Everything a project needs, under one roof.

Our full scope of in-house services includes unmatched expertise in the areas of site identification, land acquisition, land use planning and government approvals, development and construction, property management, and maintenance services.

Site Identification

We continually scout the GTA for high-potential parcels, underused retail, and assets ripe for repositioning — often before they're publicly listed.

Land Acquisition

Disciplined underwriting and patient negotiation let us assemble and acquire land on terms that work for the long term.

Land-Use Planning

We shape the highest and best use for each site — densities, mix, and massing that respect both the market and the surrounding community.

Government Approvals

Decades of experience navigating municipal planning, zoning, and approvals — turning complex processes into shovel-ready sites.

Development & Construction Management

We manage design, consultants, trades, schedule, and budget end-to-end — delivering quality on time and on plan.

Leasing & Tenant Relations

We curate a tenant mix that anchors each centre, pairing national brands with local convenience to keep traffic and value high.

Property Management

Active, in-house management keeps assets performing — responsive to tenants, attentive to detail, focused on long-term returns.

Maintenance

From parking lots to storefronts, our maintenance teams protect the look, safety, and value of every property we own.

Revitalization

We breathe new life into tired shopping centres — re-tenanting, re-merchandising, and reinvesting to restore their place in the community.

Our Process

From raw land to a thriving destination.

A clear path most projects follow — though, owning what we build, our work is never really "done."

Identify & Acquire

Source the site, underwrite the opportunity, and assemble the land with a long-term view.

Plan & Approve

Design the highest and best use, then secure the planning and municipal approvals to realize it.

Build & Lease

Manage construction to a higher standard while curating the anchor and convenience tenant mix.

Manage & Sustain

Own, operate, and maintain the asset — protecting value and community for decades.
