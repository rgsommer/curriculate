1. Who we are

InSite is a school management platform (student information system and learning management system) provided to schools (“Schools”) as a subscription service. In this policy, “InSite,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the operator of the InSite service. “You” refers to a user of InSite — a School administrator, teacher, student, or parent/guardian.

2. Scope & our role

This policy explains how InSite handles personal information processed through the platform. For most student, family, and staff records, the School is the data controller / institution responsible for the records, and InSite acts as a service provider processing that information on the School’s behalf and under its instructions and agreement with us. Where InSite collects information directly for its own account and billing purposes, InSite is the controller for that limited information.

4. How we use information

  • To provide the platform’s functions: records, gradebook, attendance, reporting, communication, graduation tracking, and Ministry reporting.
  • To authenticate users and enforce role-based access (administrators, teachers, students, parents).
  • To generate report cards, transcripts, and required government submissions (e.g., OnSIS) on the School’s instruction.
  • To secure the service, maintain audit logs, prevent abuse, and provide support.
  • To administer subscriptions and billing with the School.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use student personal information for advertising.

5. Legal & regulatory framework

InSite is designed to support Ontario schools’ obligations, including:

  • FIPPA / MFIPPA — Ontario’s public-sector freedom-of-information and privacy legislation governing education records.
  • PHIPA-equivalent handling for health information (allergies, medication, etc.): restricted access, minimal exposure, and audit logging.
  • Ontario Student Record (OSR) guideline for retention and access of student records.

Schools outside Ontario remain responsible for compliance with their own applicable laws; InSite provides the controls (role-based access, residency, audit) to support them.

6. Data residency

InSite hosts its primary database and storage in a Canadian region to support data-residency expectations under Ontario privacy law. Some sub-processors (below) may process limited data (e.g., authentication metadata) in other regions; we choose providers with appropriate safeguards.

7. Sharing & sub-processors

We share personal information only as needed to run the service:

  • Supabase — managed database, authentication, and storage (hosting provider).
  • Google — optional “Sign in with Google” authentication (we receive your name, email, and a Google account identifier).
  • Email/SMS delivery providers, where the School enables notifications.
  • Authorities or the Ministry of Education, where the School directs a required submission.

We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own marketing.

8. Security

  • Row-Level Security in the database — teachers see only their classes; parents see only their own children; students see only their own records.
  • Encryption in transit and at rest; access controls that compartmentalize health, counselling, and discipline notes.
  • Comprehensive audit logging of sensitive views and changes.
  • Backups and tested restore procedures.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect information using industry-standard measures.

9. Retention

Records are retained for as long as the School maintains its relationship with InSite and as required by the School and applicable law (including the OSR guideline). On termination, we return or delete School data per the School agreement, subject to legal retention requirements and backup cycles.

10. Your rights & access

Because the School controls most records, requests to access or correct student or family information are generally directed to the School, which can fulfil them within InSite. We assist Schools in responding to such requests. For information InSite holds as controller (e.g., account/billing), contact us using the details below.

11. Children’s information

InSite is used by schools to process information about students, including minors, at the direction of the School and consistent with the School’s authority and parental consent practices. Student accounts and data are provisioned by the School; InSite does not knowingly collect student information directly from children outside the School-directed context.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Material changes will be communicated to Schools. The “last updated” date above reflects the latest revision.
