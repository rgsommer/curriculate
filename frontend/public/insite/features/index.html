Built for Ontario

Compliance baked in, not bolted on.

InSite encodes the rules teachers and administrators already follow — so the marks, report cards and submissions come out right.

Growing Success — achievement levels

L4 80–100% — surpasses the provincial standard
L3 70–79% — meets the standard (the target)
L2 60–69% — approaches the standard
L1 50–59% — below the standard
R below 50% — remediation required

Final marks apply the 70% term / 30% final split. The six Learning Skills (E/G/S/N) are reported separately from achievement.

OSSD diploma tracking

  • ✓ 30 credits — cohort-aware compulsory split (18+12, or 17+13 for Fall 2024+)
  • ✓ OSSLT literacy requirement
  • ✓ 40 community-involvement hours, tracked live
  • ✓ 2 online learning credits (2020–21 cohort+)
  • ✓ OnSIS class-enrolment & final marks, keyed to the OEN
  • ✓ Three report-card formats: Kindergarten, Elementary & Secondary
Architecture & privacy

A platform you can trust with student data.

A modern three-tier web platform — a shared Postgres database, a secured API, and responsive role-based interfaces.

🇨🇦

Canadian data residency

Hosted in a Canadian region to keep student data in Canada and ease FIPPA/MFIPPA compliance.

🔐

Row-level security

"Teachers see their classes, parents see their children" is enforced in the database itself.

🪪

Single sign-on

One login per person via Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 — no extra password to manage.

📋

Audit logging

Comprehensive logs on sensitive views and changes, with encryption in transit and at rest.

♿

Accessible (AODA)

Interfaces target WCAG-based AODA requirements — important for a public parent portal.

🗄

Preserve, don't discard

Migrates 25 years of data and institutional rules — InSite grown up, not a replacement to relearn.
