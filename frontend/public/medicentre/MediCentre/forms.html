Patient forms & resources

Save time at your visit — download, complete, and bring the forms you need. Forms with a fee are flagged below.

Uninsured services & fees: Many forms and letters are not covered by OHIP. Fees vary by form and physician — reception will confirm any charge with you before work begins. Payment is due before the completed form is released.
