What we do

Comprehensive primary care, one trusted home

From routine check-ups to chronic-condition management, our team supports patients of every age through life's everyday and unexpected health needs.

🩺

Family Medicine

Primary care for adults and families — routine check-ups, prescription management, and ongoing health follow-up.

🚶

Walk-In Clinic

Same-day care for non-emergency concerns during regular clinic hours — no appointment required.

⏱️

Urgent Care

Faster access for acute issues that need attention sooner than a routine appointment — assessed and triaged by our team.

📞

Telehealth (Phone Visits)

Many follow-ups and routine concerns can be handled by phone — no need to come into the clinic.

🧠

Psychiatry (on-site)

A visiting psychiatrist holds clinic Monday through Thursday, 9 AM – 3 PM, on referral from your family doctor.

🦴

Rheumatology (on-site)

A visiting rheumatologist holds clinic on Fridays, 9 AM – 5 PM, on referral from your family doctor.

How appointments work

Care that fits your schedule

Request an appointment online in under two minutes. We'll match you with the right physician and time, and confirm everything by email or text.

1

Tell us about your visit

Share a few details about your symptoms, preferred timing, and whether you'd like an in-person or virtual visit.

2

We find the right slot

Our team — or our automated scheduler — matches you with the next available time that fits your preferences.

3

Confirmed in your inbox

You'll receive a confirmation right away, plus helpful reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before your visit.
