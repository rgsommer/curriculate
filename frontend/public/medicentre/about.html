About St. George Medical Centre

Our clinic

A community medical centre in Waterdown

St. George Medical Centre Waterdown is a community clinic at 250 Dundas St E in the heart of Waterdown, Ontario. We provide family medicine, walk-in care, and urgent appointments to patients across Waterdown and the surrounding areas.

Our family physicians see both rostered patients and walk-in patients during regular clinic hours, with later hours on Thursdays. We also host a visiting psychiatrist Monday through Thursday and a visiting rheumatologist on Fridays, both by referral.

Telehealth (phone) visits are available for many follow-up and routine concerns. To request an appointment, use the online booking form or call us at (289) 895-7862.

Our philosophy

How we care for patients

Four principles guide every visit at St. George Medical Centre.

🤝

Continuity of care

Whenever possible, you see your own physician. They know your history, your family, and the context behind your concerns — which means better decisions and fewer repeated conversations.

⏱️

Time to listen

We aim to give every visit the time it needs — listening fully before offering a plan, and explaining the reasoning behind our recommendations.

🛡️

Prevention first

Annual physicals, screening programs, chronic-condition follow-ups, and immunizations are the backbone of our practice. We work to keep small concerns from becoming big ones.

🧭

Clarity & honesty

We explain options in plain language, discuss what we don't know, and never pressure patients into tests or treatments that aren't right for them.

Our policies

How visits work at our clinic

📅 Booked appointments

We strongly encourage booked appointments for routine, follow-up, and non-urgent care. You can request an appointment online — our team will match you with the next time that fits your preferences and confirm by email or text.

Book online →

🚶 Walk-in care

Walk-ins are welcomed during regular clinic hours for non-emergency concerns. Wait times depend on volume — please call ahead or check in at reception when you arrive.

📞 Telehealth (phone) visits

Many follow-ups and routine concerns can be handled by phone. Choose "Phone (telehealth)" when requesting your appointment.

🚨 After-hours & emergencies

For after-hours non-emergency advice, call Telehealth Ontario at 811. For medical emergencies, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Have a question we haven't answered?

Our reception team is happy to help — by phone, by email, or in person.
