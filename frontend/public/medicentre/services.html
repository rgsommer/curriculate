FAQ

Questions we hear often

Are you accepting new patients?

Whether individual physicians are accepting new patients changes over time. Please call (289) 895-7862 to confirm current availability.

What's covered by OHIP?

Most medical services are covered by OHIP. Some items — such as forms, letters, and certain travel-related services — are not. Reception will confirm any fee with you before work begins.

Do you have a walk-in clinic?

Yes. Walk-in patients are seen during regular clinic hours alongside booked appointments. Wait times depend on volume.

Can I have a phone visit instead?

Telehealth (phone) visits are available for many follow-ups and routine concerns. Just choose "Phone (telehealth)" when requesting your appointment.
