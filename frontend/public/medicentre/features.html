A modern clinic experience, designed around you

St. George Medical Centre pairs the warm, personal care of a community clinic with a modern digital platform that saves you time and keeps you informed.

🗓️

Online appointment booking

Request an appointment in under two minutes — pick a specific doctor or "Any available", tell us about your visit, and choose times that fit your schedule.

Try it →
💻

Virtual visits

Many follow-ups, prescription renewals, and mental-health visits can happen by phone or secure video — no need to come into the clinic.

🔔

Automatic reminders

Friendly email or SMS reminders go out 24 hours and 2 hours before every appointment, with the address, parking info, and anything you need to bring.

🤖

AI-personalised prep

If your visit needs special preparation — fasting bloodwork, an empty bladder, a list of meds — your reminder explains exactly what to do, in plain language.

👨‍👩‍👧

Booking for family

Parents, spouses, and caregivers can book on behalf of someone else — we'll send updates to the booker and the patient where appropriate.

🚨

Urgent request triage

Marking your visit as "Urgent" puts it at the top of the reception queue so we can offer the soonest available time, often the same day.

📋

New-patient onboarding

Tell us about your previous doctor, current medications, allergies, and history during booking — so your first visit can focus on you, not paperwork.

🔐

PHIPA-compliant privacy

Your personal health information is handled in line with Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act. Access is logged and reviewed.

📅

Outlook calendar sync

Once a reception team member confirms your appointment, it lands directly in your physician's clinic calendar — no double-booking, no lost notes.

Live integration rolling out in 2026.

For our patients

Less waiting, more care

Every digital feature we build serves a single purpose: more of your visit is spent actually with your physician. Less time on hold, less paperwork to fill out at the desk, and fewer surprises about what to bring or how to prepare.

  • Book online from your phone, 24/7.
  • Switch a visit to virtual without rebooking.
  • Get reminders with exactly what you need.
  • Family members can help manage care.
