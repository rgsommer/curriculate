Discipleship

What Is Baptism?

A short answer to a question that comes up often.

Baptism is a public way of saying: "I belong to Jesus." It doesn't save you — Jesus does that — but it's the outward marker of an inward reality. New life. Cleansing. A fresh start. A family.

What we practise

At Flamborough Baptist, we baptize by immersion: going under the water and coming back up, picturing dying with Christ and rising with him to new life. We baptize believers — anyone who has put their trust in Jesus and is ready to publicly own that.

Who's it for?

If you've decided to follow Jesus and you haven't been baptized as a believer yet, this is your next step. Age isn't the question; faith is.

How does it happen?

We hold baptism services a few times a year. Before the day, you'll meet with a pastor to share your story and walk through what baptism means. On the day, you'll have the chance to share a few words with the church, then go under the water in front of your church family.
