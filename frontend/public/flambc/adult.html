Ministries

Adult Ministries

Where deeper friendships and growing faith meet.

Small groups

Small groups are where the church family becomes personal. Most groups meet weekly or biweekly in homes around Waterdown — sharing meals, opening Scripture, praying for one another, and showing up when life gets hard.

Studies

Through the year we run topical studies, book studies, and ministry-equipping classes. Watch the calendar or ask the office what's currently running.

Men & Women

Periodic gatherings, breakfasts, and retreats specifically for the men and women of our church. Different seasons bring different things — keep an eye on the announcements.
