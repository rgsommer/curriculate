Youth Ministries

The Gathering — Thursdays 7:00–9:00 pm.

The Gathering is our youth ministry for grades 7 through 12. Thursday nights are part hangout, part deep conversation, part Scripture, and always loud. Bring a friend.

What to expect

  • Games and food (always food)
  • A short Bible-centred message
  • Small groups for honest conversation
  • Seasonal retreats, service projects, and fun nights

Led by Alex Burns, Director of Student Ministries — alex@flambc.org.
