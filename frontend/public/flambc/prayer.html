Prayer Request

Anonymous or named — we'd be honoured to pray with you.

One of our six values is prayer. Sometimes we're praying for big things; sometimes for small daily ones. Either way, we believe God listens, and we'd love to pray alongside you.

Submissions go to a small, confidential prayer team. If you'd like a pastor to reach back out, leave your contact info — otherwise, prayer-only is great too.
