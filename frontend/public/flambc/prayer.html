Prayer

Our Elder Team would be glad to pray with you.

Do you need prayer?

Whatever you're carrying — a hard diagnosis, a job loss, a child's struggle, a quiet ache, or something joyful you'd love a few people to thank God for with you — our Elder Team would consider it a privilege to pray for you.

Your request will be held with care. We honour your privacy: only the prayer team sees what you submit, and nothing leaves that small group without your say-so.

A reminder we hold close as a prayer team — Colossians 4:6.
