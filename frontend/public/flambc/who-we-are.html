About

Who We Are

A Baptist church in Waterdown, Ontario, learning to follow Jesus together.

Our story, in short

Flamborough Baptist Church is a community of ordinary people pursuing an extraordinary God. We meet on Sunday mornings to worship, hear from Scripture, and pray — and through the week we live into what that means as friends, neighbours, parents, students, and workers.

If you walked in on a Sunday, you'd find singing, a Bible-centred message, kids' programs running alongside the service, and coffee in hand on the way out. Casual on the surface; serious about Jesus underneath.

What we believe

We hold to the core convictions of historic Baptist Christianity: the authority of Scripture, salvation by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, the priesthood of every believer, baptism by immersion as a public sign of new life, and the local church as the gathered family where faith is lived out.

How we worship

Sunday services are about an hour and fifteen minutes — singing, prayer, Scripture, and a teaching message that walks through books of the Bible over time. Children stay with their families for the start of the service before heading to age-appropriate programming.

Our six core values

  • Community — life shared, not just attended.
  • Generosity — open hands with our time, gifts, and resources.
  • Missional — sent to our neighbours, our city, and the world.
  • Prayer — listening before we move.
  • Transformation — slow, real growth into the likeness of Jesus.
  • Worship — heart, mind, and voice turned toward God.

Plan a visit

You don't need to know anyone or wear anything in particular. Park anywhere, walk in the front doors, and someone will say hello. Service starts at 10:00 am.
