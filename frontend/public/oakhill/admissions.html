Admissions

Now welcoming families for 2026–27.

Our admissions process is unhurried by design. It is a conversation — between two families, ours and yours — about whether Oak Hill is the right place for your children to grow.

Oak Hill Academy is a biblically-based, religious organization that supports families who choose to give their children a Christian education rooted in the school's Confession of Faith and Affirmation of Core Family Values.

We welcome students from families who share these convictions — and from families who do not, with the clear understanding that every student will be taught all aspects of the curriculum, including the school's beliefs.

Seats are currently available in Grades 4 through 10. The application process is below.

The Application Process

Nine steps from first inquiry to first day.

Get to know us.

Read our mission and distinctives, our academic philosophy, and our educational priorities. The values described there will shape everything that follows.

Read three foundational pieces.

  • Our Confession of Faith and Affirmation of Core Family Values
  • Dorothy Sayers, The Lost Tools of Learning
  • Charlotte Mason, 20 Principles of Education

Schedule a school visit.

Email admin@oakhillhamilton.ca to arrange a time to come see Oak Hill in motion.

Submit a new family application.

  • Student Admissions Application
  • Signed Confession of Faith and Affirmation of Core Family Values
  • Recent report card
  • $500 non-refundable application fee
  • Parent Volunteer Survey

Family interview.

Both parents (and the prospective student) meet with the Principal and members of the leadership team for a conversation about your family, your hopes, and Oak Hill.

Student assessment.

Reading, writing, and math placement assessments help us understand where to begin and how to serve your child well.

Student audit of intended class.

Three to five days in the classroom at the school's discretion. A chance for the student — and for us — to see whether Oak Hill is the right fit.

Notification in writing.

Following the assessment and audit, families are notified of the admissions decision in writing.

Enrolment.

A $500 non-refundable family deposit, due within one week of acceptance, secures the seat. The deposit is applied toward tuition.

Bursaries

Tuition assistance is available.

Oak Hill Academy is committed to making a classical Christian education accessible to families for whom full tuition would be a barrier. Bursaries are awarded based on demonstrated financial need.

Applications are processed through FAST, an independent third-party service. The bursary application opens in January each year; the first round closes March 31.
