Welcome to Oak Hill

A community of families whose imaginations have been captured.

From Moses and Homer to Dante, John Calvin, and Teresa of Ávila — down through Dorothy Sayers and C.S. Lewis — the end of a classical Christian education is not merely a career, but the cultivation of the human spirit.

We desire to see our children strive to be sages and saints, to love God and neighbour, to know their heritage and home, and to carry their child-like wonder with them all their lives.

Radix coronam nutrit. — The root nourishes the crown.

The Three Stages

An education shaped to how children grow.

The classical Trivium — Grammar, Logic, and Rhetoric — meets students where they are and forms them year by year into thoughtful, articulate, virtuous adults.

i.

Grammar

Grades 1 – 4

The poll-parrot years. Children memorize joyfully — facts, songs, Scripture, Latin chants — and fill the storehouse of imagination with stories and great art.

ii.

Logic

Grades 5 – 8

The pert and questioning years. Students learn to think well: to follow an argument, to spot a fallacy, to defend a thesis, to wrestle with ideas.

iii.

Rhetoric

Grades 9 – 12

The poetic years. Students learn to speak and write with clarity, confidence, and charm — and to apply their formed minds to a vocation worth giving themselves to.
